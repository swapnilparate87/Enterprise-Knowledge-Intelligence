SUMMARY: Quick guide to Northstar Systems security awareness training, covering joiner and annual
courses in NorthLearn, quarterly phishing simulations, the three-failure retraining rule, how to
report suspicious messages and the everyday habits every employee and contractor must follow.

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - Northstar Systems is a fictional company. This document exists for development,
testing and demonstration only and is not real legal, employment, security or compliance advice.


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------

This guide gives employees and contractors of Northstar Systems Private Limited a short, practical
summary of security awareness training: what training must be completed, when, how phishing
simulations work, what happens after repeated failures, and the daily habits that keep company and
customer data safe. It applies to all employees (Bengaluru, Mumbai and remote) and to contractors
who hold a corporate identity in NorthID. It is a quick guide; the binding rules sit in the
Information Security Policy (SEC-001), the Code of Conduct (see SEC-006) and the access, device and
data policies they refer to.


2. REQUIRED TRAINING AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------

  Training                                         | Who                                         | Deadline                                         | Where
  -------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------+----------------
  Security Foundations course                      | New joiners and contractors                 | Within 14 days of joining                        | NorthLearn
  Annual security refresher                        | All employees and contractors               | Once every year, by the date shown in NorthLearn | NorthLearn
  Quarterly phishing simulation                    | All employees with a corporate mailbox      | Runs every quarter without advance notice        | Corporate email
  Retraining module                                | Anyone who fails 3 simulations in 12 months | Within 10 working days of assignment             | NorthLearn

New joiners must complete the Security Foundations course within 14 days of their joining date. The
course is assigned automatically in NorthLearn when the HR onboarding tasks in NorthPeople are
created (see HR-005). Completion is tracked, and the GRC Manager's team reports overdue completions
to department heads every month.


3. THE SECURITY FOUNDATIONS COURSE
----------------------------------

The course takes about 90 minutes and can be paused and resumed. It covers:

  - Recognising phishing, smishing (SMS) and voice-call scams.
  - Passwords, multi-factor authentication and NorthVault.
  - Data classification: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted.
  - Safe use of laptops, mobile devices and public networks.
  - Acceptable use, including the Approved AI Tools list.
  - How to report an incident and why speed matters.

A pass mark of 80% on the final quiz is required. A learner may retake the quiz as many times as
needed; the course is complete only when the pass mark is reached. Contractors who cannot access
NorthLearn must ask their Northstar sponsor to raise a NorthDesk ticket so an account can be
provisioned.

Example: A new Software Engineer in Bengaluru joins on 3 June. The 14-day deadline is 17 June. If
the course is still incomplete on 17 June, the learner's manager is notified, and access to some
non-essential systems may be limited by the Security Desk until the course is finished.


4. ANNUAL REFRESHER
-------------------

Every employee and contractor must complete the annual refresher, a shorter course of about 45
minutes that covers changes in threats, policy updates and lessons from real incidents. NorthLearn
shows the due date on the learner's dashboard and sends reminders 30 days, 14 days and 3 days before
it. Anyone who joined within the last 6 months and completed Security Foundations is credited for
that year's refresher.


5. PHISHING SIMULATIONS
-----------------------

The GRC team sends simulated phishing messages to employees every quarter. The messages imitate real
attack styles, such as a fake courier notice, a fake HR payroll update or a fake NorthID password
prompt. Timing and content are not announced in advance.

An employee is counted as having "failed" a simulation if they click the link, open the attachment
or enter any information on the landing page. Reporting the message with the Report Phish button
counts as a pass. Ignoring the message and deleting it is neutral: it is not a failure but it does
not help the Security Desk, so reporting is always preferred.


5.1 The three-failure rule

An employee who fails 3 simulations within any rolling 12 months must complete the mandatory
retraining module in NorthLearn and have a conversation with their manager about secure working
habits. The conversation is supportive and not disciplinary; its purpose is to find out whether
workload, a confusing mailbox setup or lack of understanding is the cause. Repeated failures after
retraining may be raised with HR under the Code of Conduct, but a simulation result alone does not
lead to a warning.

Example: Priya in Customer Success clicks the simulated "courier delivery" link in January, the
simulated "payslip" link in April, and the simulated "NorthID MFA reset" link in October. The third
failure falls inside 12 months, so NorthLearn assigns the retraining module and the Security Desk
informs her manager to schedule the conversation.


5.2 Privacy of results

Individual simulation results are visible only to the GRC team, the employee and, for the three-
failure rule, the employee's manager. Department-level statistics shared with leadership never name
individuals.


6. REPORTING SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY
--------------------------------

  - Suspicious email: click the Report Phish button in the mail client. Do not forward the message
    to colleagues.
  - Any other suspected incident, such as a lost laptop, a strange login prompt, a wrong-recipient
    email or malware: contact the Security Desk immediately on ext. 5555 or at security@northstar-
    systems.example.
  - A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Security Desk within 1 hour of discovery. Late
    reporting makes remote wipe and customer risk assessment harder.
  - You will never be penalised for reporting in good faith, even if it turns out to be a false
    alarm or you clicked something by mistake. Early reporting is always better than silence.


7. EVERYDAY HABITS CHECKLIST
----------------------------

  - Use a password of at least 14 characters, store it in NorthVault and never reuse it across
    systems. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; change one immediately if you suspect
    compromise.
  - Approve MFA prompts in NorthID only for logins you started yourself. If you receive a prompt you
    did not trigger, deny it and report it.
  - Lock your screen whenever you step away; the device locks automatically after 5 minutes.
  - Use only company laptops for company work. Do not use personal laptops.
  - Never put Confidential or Restricted data into external AI tools. Use only tools on the Approved
    AI Tools list.
  - Do not send Restricted data through personal email or messaging apps. Use approved sharing tools
    only.
  - Escort visitors and challenge anyone in the office without a badge.
  - Connect to internal applications through NorthTunnel when working remotely, and avoid public Wi-
    Fi for company work unless the VPN is active.
  - Do not install unauthorised software or plug unknown USB drives into your laptop.


8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------

What if I am on long leave when the deadline falls? The deadline is extended by the length of
approved leave. Tell the GRC team through NorthDesk and complete the course within 14 days of
returning.

Do contractors have to take the same training? Yes. Contractors with a corporate identity follow the
same schedule and the same three-failure rule.

Can I skip the refresher because I work in security? No. Everyone completes it. Security staff may
additionally take advanced modules.

I clicked on a real phishing email. What now? Disconnect from the network if you can, then call the
Security Desk on ext. 5555 straight away. Do not try to fix it yourself and do not wait to see if
anything happens.


9. RESPONSIBILITIES
-------------------

  - Employees and contractors must complete assigned training on time and report incidents promptly.
  - Managers should give staff time to complete training and must hold the conversation required
    after three failed simulations.
  - The GRC Manager owns the programme, content, pass marks and reporting.
  - The Security Desk runs the simulations and handles reported messages.
  - HR (People Team) assigns the joiner course through NorthPeople onboarding tasks.
