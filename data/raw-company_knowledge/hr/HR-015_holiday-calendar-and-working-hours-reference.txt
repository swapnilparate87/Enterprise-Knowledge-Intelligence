SUMMARY: Reference sheet for the fictional Northstar Systems holiday calendar for calendar year
2026, covering 12 company holidays, 6 optional floating holidays with selection rules, standard
working hours, late-mark rules, hybrid days, core hours, support coverage on holidays and office
opening times.

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - Northstar Systems is a fictional company. This document exists for development,
testing and demonstration only and is not real legal, employment, security or compliance advice.


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------

This reference lists the Northstar Systems Private Limited holiday calendar for calendar year 2026,
the standard working hours and the attendance rules that go with them. All holiday names and dates
in this document are fictional and are used only for Northstar's internal planning; they do not
claim to match any real public calendar. It applies to all employees in Bengaluru, Mumbai and remote
locations. Leave types, accrual and approval rules are in HR-001; this document gives the calendar
and hours that those rules refer to.

Where a holiday is observed by statute in a given state, the more favourable rule for the employee
applies. Any legal reference here is illustrative; to be verified by Legal before external use.


2. DEFINITIONS
--------------

  - Company holiday: a paid day off on which all Northstar offices are closed and no work is
    expected, other than approved on-call or customer support coverage.
  - Floating holiday: an optional holiday chosen by the employee from the list in section 4. Each
    employee may take 2 floating holidays per calendar year.
  - Working day: Monday to Friday, excluding company holidays.
  - Late mark: arrival after 9:30 IST, recorded by badge or NorthPeople check-in.
  - Core collaboration hours: 11:00 to 16:00 IST, when employees are expected to be available for
    meetings.


3. COMPANY HOLIDAYS 2026
------------------------

Northstar observes 12 company holidays in calendar year 2026. All of them fall on weekdays, so no
day is lost to a weekend.

  No. | Date             | Day       | Holiday
  ----+------------------+-----------+--------------------------------------------------------
  1   | 1 January 2026   | Thursday  | New Year's Day
  2   | 26 January 2026  | Monday    | Republic Day
  3   | 4 March 2026     | Wednesday | Spring Festival
  4   | 3 April 2026     | Friday    | Spring Observance Day
  5   | 1 May 2026       | Friday    | Labour Day
  6   | 14 August 2026   | Friday    | Independence Day (observed, as 15 August is a Saturday)
  7   | 2 October 2026   | Friday    | Gandhi Jayanti
  8   | 20 October 2026  | Tuesday   | Autumn Festival

  10  | 13 November 2026 | Friday    | Festival of Lights, Day 2
  11  | 23 November 2026 | Monday    | Founders' Day
  12  | 25 December 2026 | Friday    | Year-End Day

Notes on company holidays:

  - Founders' Day marks the founding of Northstar in 2012 and is a company-only holiday.
  - If a company holiday is moved by the company, the Chief People Officer announces the change at
    least 30 calendar days in advance by email and in NorthPeople.
  - A company holiday that falls inside an approved leave period is not counted as leave.
  - Employees who are required to work on a company holiday, for example on-call engineers or
    support staff, receive compensatory off, which must be used within 60 days.


4. OPTIONAL FLOATING HOLIDAYS 2026
----------------------------------

Each employee may choose 2 floating holidays from the following list of 6. They are in addition to
the 12 company holidays.

  No. | Date             | Day       | Optional holiday
  ----+------------------+-----------+---------------------
  F1  | 14 January 2026  | Wednesday | Harvest Festival
  F2  | 20 March 2026    | Friday    | Spring New Year
  F3  | 29 May 2026      | Friday    | Midyear Observance
  F4  | 7 September 2026 | Monday    | Late Summer Festival
  F5  | 16 October 2026  | Friday    | Autumn Eve
  F6  | 24 December 2026 | Thursday  | Year-End Eve

Rules for floating holidays:

  1. Choose in NorthPeople under Leave, then Floating Holidays. An employee should make the choice
     by 31 January 2026, and new joiners within 30 days of joining.
  2. Only 2 floating holidays may be taken in the calendar year. Unused floating holidays lapse on
     31 December and cannot be encashed or carried forward.
  3. A choice can be changed up to 7 calendar days before the chosen date.
  4. Floating holidays follow the manager approval rule: the manager must approve or reject within 2
     working days in NorthPeople or the request escalates to the department head. Managers should
     approve unless a customer commitment or on-call duty makes it impossible, in which case the
     manager offers another date from the list that is still available.
  5. Floating holidays do not reduce the balance of Casual Leave, Sick Leave or Earned Leave.

Example: An employee at the Powai office picks F1 (14 January) and F6 (24 December) in NorthPeople
on 20 January. Later she asks to swap F6 for F5 (16 October). If she makes the change on or before 9
October, which is 7 calendar days before 16 October, the swap is allowed.


5. STANDARD WORKING HOURS
-------------------------

The standard working day is 9:30 to 18:30 IST, which includes a 1 hour lunch break. The working week
is Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs unless a role has an approved roster.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Meetings with participants from more than one team
should be scheduled inside core hours. Employees may adjust their start or end times with the
manager's agreement, provided they are present during core hours and complete the standard daily
hours.


5.1 Late marks

The late-arrival grace is up to 3 late marks per month, each of up to 30 minutes. The 4th late mark
in a month is deducted as half-day casual leave. An arrival more than 30 minutes after 9:30 IST is
treated as a half-day or loss-of-pay absence unless the manager approves the exception in
NorthPeople.

Example: An employee arrives at 9:45, 9:50 and 10:00 on three days in a month and uses all 3 late
marks. On a fourth day in the same month, she arrives at 9:40. A half-day of casual leave (0.5 day)
is deducted from her Casual Leave balance of 8 days per calendar year.


5.2 Hybrid working

The hybrid default is 3 days in office per week. The anchor days are Tuesday, Wednesday and
Thursday, when all hybrid teams are expected to be in office to support collaboration. Employees may
choose the other in-office day or days with the manager's agreement. Fully remote roles must be
approved by the VP of the function and the Chief People Officer.


5.3 Working from another location

Working from a location other than the registered city for more than 10 working days in a year needs
HR and Security approval. Employees must apply in advance through NorthDesk and HR, and the request
includes dates, location, network arrangement and confirmation that corporate data will be accessed
only through NorthTunnel with MFA via NorthID.


5.4 Remote working allowances

Fully remote employees receive a work-from-home allowance of INR 1,500 per month to cover internet
and utilities. A one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to INR 15,000 may be claimed once
every 3 years, with receipts, through NorthPay.


6. OFFICES, SUPPORT COVERAGE AND HOLIDAYS
-----------------------------------------

  - Offices in Bengaluru (Northstar Tower, Whitefield) and Mumbai (Powai) are open from 8:00 to
    20:00 on working days. Weekend access needs manager approval via NorthDesk.
  - Offices are closed on company holidays. Visitors are not scheduled on those days.
  - Standard-tier customer support hours are 9:00 to 19:00 IST Monday to Friday, excluding company
    holidays. Premier and Enterprise customers have 24x7 coverage for P1 and P2 issues, including on
    company holidays, provided through a roster of Customer Success staff. Staff on the roster
    receive compensatory off.
  - Production on-call continues through all holidays. The weekly on-call rotation changes on Monday
    at 10:00 IST; if the Monday is a company holiday, the handover still happens at 10:00 IST by
    call or chat.
  - Payroll is credited on the last working day of the month. If the last day of a month is a
    company holiday or weekend, payroll is credited on the preceding working day.
  - Payment runs to vendors happen every Tuesday and Friday; when one of these is a company holiday,
    the run moves to the next working day.

Example: 31 October 2026 is a Saturday, so October payroll is credited on Friday 30 October 2026. In
contrast, 24 December 2026 is a Thursday and an optional holiday, so the standard payroll and
payment runs are not affected by it.


7. PLANNING NOTES FOR MANAGERS
------------------------------

  - Review the holiday calendar before setting sprint dates, release dates and customer commitments.
    Production deployments are normally in the window Tuesday to Thursday, so the Autumn Festival on
    Tuesday 20 October reduces the available window in that week.
  - Avoid scheduling mandatory meetings on floating holiday dates where many team members may have
    chosen them. NorthPeople shows the floating holiday choices of a team to the manager.
  - Plan coverage for 12 and 13 November (two consecutive holidays) and for the period from 24 to 25
    December in advance. Teams with customer commitments must publish a coverage roster at least 10
    working days before.
  - Employees may combine floating holidays and Earned Leave to make long breaks; Earned Leave of 5
    or more consecutive working days needs 10 working days' notice.
