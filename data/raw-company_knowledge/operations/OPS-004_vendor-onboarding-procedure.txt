===========================

Department:                 Operations and Procurement

Version:                    3.0
Status:                     APPROVED
Effective / last reviewed:  2025-10-09
Audience:                   Budget owners, managers and Finance/Procurement staff
Related documents:          OPS-003, SEC-011, OPS-009, OPS-005

SUMMARY: Step-by-step procedure for onboarding a new vendor into the NorthBuy vendor master at
Northstar Systems, covering required documents, security assessment for vendors touching
Confidential or Restricted data, timelines, payment terms and common rejection reasons.

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - Northstar Systems is a fictional company. This document exists for development,
testing and demonstration only and is not real legal, employment, security or compliance advice.


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------

This procedure explains how a new vendor, supplier or service provider is onboarded into the
NorthBuy vendor master at Northstar Systems Private Limited. It applies to every vendor that
Northstar intends to pay, regardless of purchase value, including consultants, software and cloud
providers, facility service companies and staffing agencies. It is used by budget owners who request
a vendor, by Procurement staff who run the onboarding, and by Finance staff who release the first
payment.

The rule behind the procedure is simple: a vendor must be onboarded in the NorthBuy vendor master
before a purchase order (PO) is issued. No commitment may be made to a vendor that is not in the
master, and invoices from non-onboarded vendors are not paid. Utilities and pre-approved statutory
payments are the only exceptions and are listed in OPS-001.

This procedure does not cover the purchase decision itself (quotations, RFPs and approval limits are
in OPS-001 and OPS-003) or contract drafting and renewal (OPS-005). Invoice handling after
onboarding is described in OPS-009, which is currently a draft.


2. DEFINITIONS
--------------

  - Vendor master: the controlled list of approved vendors held in NorthBuy, with bank details, tax
    identifiers, risk tier and contract links.
  - Requester: the budget owner or employee who needs the vendor.
  - Complete document set: all documents listed in section 4 for the vendor's risk category, in
    legible form and signed where required.
  - Vendor risk tier: Critical, High, Moderate or Low, assigned by data access and system
    criticality as described in SEC-011.
  - MSME vendor: a vendor holding a valid micro, small or medium enterprise registration
    (illustrative; to be verified by Legal before external use).


3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
-----------------------------

  Role                       | Responsibility
  ---------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Requester                  | Raises the vendor request in NorthBuy, supplies business justification and a single point of contact at the vendor
  Procurement Desk           | Checks documents, creates the vendor record, runs duplicate and sanctions checks, completes onboarding
  Security (via IT/Security) | Reviews the security questionnaire and assurance report for vendors that access Confidential or Restricted data
  Legal                      | Reviews the Data Processing Agreement and any non-standard NDA terms
  Finance Controller         | Verifies bank details and releases the record for payment


4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
---------------------

Every vendor must provide the following documents before onboarding can start:

  1. PAN (Permanent Account Number) card or certificate.
  2. GSTIN registration certificate, where the vendor is GST registered.
  3. A cancelled cheque or bank proof showing account name, number and IFSC code.
  4. A signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) on the Northstar template, or approved vendor-side NDA
     reviewed by Legal.
  5. A signed conflict-of-interest declaration.

In addition, a vendor that will access Confidential or Restricted data, or connect to Northstar
systems, must complete the security questionnaire. Critical and High risk vendors must also submit
an independent assurance report, for example a SOC 2 Type II report or an ISO 27001 certificate
(illustrative), before any PO is issued. If personal data will be shared, a Data Processing
Agreement (DPA) must be signed before the first data transfer.

MSME vendors should also submit their registration certificate so that the shorter payment term is
applied from the first invoice.


5. STEP-BY-STEP ONBOARDING
--------------------------

  1. The requester raises a vendor request in NorthBuy, naming the proposed service, expected annual
     spend, cost-centre code and the data the vendor will touch.
  2. The requester collects the documents in section 4 from the vendor and attaches them. A request
     without all documents is marked incomplete and returned.
  3. Procurement checks that the PAN, GSTIN and bank details are consistent (same legal name), and
     searches the vendor master for duplicates. A vendor must never be created twice under different
     spellings.
  4. Procurement assigns a provisional risk tier. If the tier is Critical or High, or if any
     Confidential or Restricted data is involved, Procurement sends the request to Security with the
     questionnaire.
  5. Security assesses the questionnaire and assurance report. The assessment SLA is 10 business
     days from the date the questionnaire is complete.
  6. Legal reviews the DPA where personal data is involved.
  7. The Finance Controller verifies the bank details by a small test credit or a call-back to a
     known contact, never to a number supplied only on the request form.
  8. Procurement activates the vendor in the NorthBuy vendor master and notifies the requester.
     Procurement completes onboarding within 5 business days of receiving a complete document set;
     security assessment time for Critical and High vendors runs in parallel and is counted
     separately.

Only after step 8 may the requester raise a purchase requisition and PO.


6. TIMELINES
------------

  Stage                                          | Target
  -----------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------
  Onboarding after complete document set         | 5 business days
  Security assessment (Critical or High vendors) | 10 business days from complete questionnaire
  Bank detail verification                       | 2 business days, within the onboarding window
  Reassessment of Critical vendors               | Annually
  Reassessment of High vendors                   | Every 2 years

Requesters should start onboarding at least 15 business days before they need the first PO for a
Critical or High vendor. Urgent requests do not change the controls; they only change the order in
which Procurement works its queue.


7. PAYMENT TERMS AND ADVANCES
-----------------------------

Standard payment terms are Net 45 days from invoice receipt. MSME-registered vendors are paid on Net
30 terms (illustrative). Payment runs happen every Tuesday and Friday. An advance payment of up to
30% of PO value is possible only for an onboarded vendor and needs CFO approval. Procurement records
agreed terms on the vendor record so that Finance applies them automatically.

Example: A Mumbai-based facilities contractor with a valid MSME certificate invoices INR 1,80,000.
Because the vendor was onboarded with the certificate attached, Finance schedules payment on Net 30
terms from invoice receipt instead of Net 45.


8. CHANGES TO VENDOR DETAILS
----------------------------

Bank detail changes are a common fraud route. A vendor asking to change bank details must send a
signed letter on letterhead and a new cancelled cheque. Procurement and the Finance Controller must
verify the request by calling a number already held in the vendor master, and the change takes
effect only after this verification. No bank detail change may be accepted by email alone. Changes
to legal name, PAN or GSTIN need a fresh review as if the vendor were new.

A vendor with no PO or payment for 24 months is marked inactive. Inactive vendors can be reactivated
by Procurement after refreshing the documents.


9. COMMON REJECTION REASONS AND EDGE CASES
------------------------------------------

  - Name on PAN does not match the bank account name: Procurement returns the request until the
    vendor provides a name-change proof or an account in the correct name.
  - Vendor will receive Restricted data but has no assurance report: onboarding stays open, but no
    PO may be issued. The requester may ask for a time-limited security exception through the CISO
    delegate; exceptions expire in at most 90 days.
  - Individual consultants without GST: may be onboarded with PAN and bank proof; tax treatment is
    decided by Finance.
  - Sole proprietors using personal accounts: allowed only for low-value services and with Finance
    Controller approval.
  - Related-party vendors: a conflict-of-interest declaration that discloses a relationship with a
    Northstar employee must be sent to the department head and Procurement; the employee must not
    take part in selecting or approving the vendor.

Example: The Customer Success department wants to engage a transcription vendor that will listen to
recorded customer calls. Because call recordings may contain customer data, Procurement classifies
the vendor as High risk, requests the security questionnaire and a DPA, and does not allow a PO
until Security has finished its assessment.
